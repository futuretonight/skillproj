Transparent Skilling Impact Score Formula

Documented, audit-ready weighted mathematical formulation

Skilling Impact Score =

0.35 × (Avg Skill Improvement %)

+ 0.25 × (Course Completion Rate %)

+ 0.25 × (Job Relevance Rate %)

+ 0.15 × (Overall Employment Rate %)

Why 35% on Skill Improvement? Ensures training institutions are judged on genuine capability gains rather than pass-through certificates.

Why 25% on Job Relevance? Discourages placing certified AI/Data students into unrelated gig/delivery roles solely to artificially inflate counts.
